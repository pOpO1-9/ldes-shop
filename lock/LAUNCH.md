## TikTok system

Post from **@ldes.ai**. Same face of the brand, new product. Do not split the audience onto a second account until Still Lock is making sales.

Cadence: **1 lock video per day for 14 days**, then 4–5 a week. Film on the real phone. The shop already shows the still with a clock on it — match that.

### Hooks (first 1.5 seconds, text on screen)

1. Your wallpaper is pretty. Try unlocking it.
2. I leave a hole for the clock.
3. This one does not slap you awake at 2am.
4. €12. Eight locks. The one from this video is in the kit.
5. Stop setting museum paintings as your lockscreen.
6. Nightstand test.
7. Before / after: clock unreadable → clock readable.
8. The lock from yesterday’s still, on a phone.

### 12 scripts (15 seconds, talk or caption)

Keep the last 2–3 seconds silent: phone on the table, lock showing. No extra Kling spend — shoot the phone.

1. Hold the phone. Wallpaper full-bleed, time unreadable. Cut to Still Lock composition. “Room for the clock.” Bio.
2. Dark room. Unlock a white-sky wallpaper. Wince. Cut to Cabin Reflection. Whisper: “dim.”
3. Scroll Pinterest locks. “Free, and they all hide the widgets.” Cut to Kit 01 grid.
4. Screen recording: Photos → Wallpaper → Lock Screen → set Mist Harbor. 12 seconds. End on 9:41.
5. “Eight locks, twelve euros. The stills you already liked.”
6. Finger pointing at the top third: “Nothing important lives here. That’s where the time goes.”
7. Split: LDES gallery piece vs the same piece in a phone bezel. “Same still. Different job.”
8. “I will not sell you a clip today. I will sell you the screen you live on.”
9. Month tease: paper calendar tearing. “This lock expires. That’s the point.” (only after Month Lock exists.)
10. Duet a pretty-but-unusable lock. No roast, just the fix.
11. Packing-slip energy: Gumroad email screenshot, then the lock on the phone. Proof it is real.
12. Stitch “what should I set as my wallpaper?” — answer with one kit, one price, one link.

Pin on every video: `Kit 01 · €12 · link in bio` until the €4 single is live. Then pin `This lock · €4`.

## What “making something of it” looks like

First useful target is not a company. It is **repeatable small sales from the same TikToks you already post.**

- Week 1: kits moving from bio traffic.
- Week 2–3: €4 singles under the videos.
- Month 2: Night Dim + first Month Lock (people buy again).
- Only then: a second TikTok, or a custom €29 lock (one pair, prompt, no €79 gallery commission).

If nothing sells after 14 lock videos, the product is not the problem yet — the click path is. Check bio link, Pages URL, and that Gumroad is the next tap, not the mint page.
