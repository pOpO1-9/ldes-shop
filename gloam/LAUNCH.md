## Why this, not 40 AliExpress gadgets

- **One page, one object.** Same discipline as LDES and Most Nights.
- **It films itself.** The gold circle on a wall is the ad. You already shoot rooms.
- **Dropshipping is the fulfillment, not the brand.** The shop is Gloam. The warehouse is invisible.

Soap and protein still need rules and reviews. This lamp is small, ships cheap, and matches the look you already know how to light.

## Do this in order (or you will refund people)

1. **Pick a supplier with EU stock** if you can. Search CJ Dropshipping / Zendrop / Spocket for “sunset projection lamp USB-C”. Filter: EU warehouse, 4.8+ , orders in the thousands.
2. **Order a sample to your own address. Pay for it.** The site photos are the *look*, not a photo of your carton. When the sample arrives: check the tilt, the USB-C, the heat, the circle on a real wall. If it’s junk, pick another listing. Do not run ads on a lamp you have not held.
3. **CE / RoHS.** If the sample has no CE mark on the lamp or the carton, do not sell it into the EU. Find a listing that does, or skip this product.
4. **Retake three photos** of *your* sample (wall circle, product, contents). Replace the files in `gloam/assets/`.
5. **Gumroad physical product** `GLOAM` · €39 · require shipping address. No file to download.
6. Paste the live URL into `config.js` → `checkoutUrl`.
7. New TikTok: **`@gloam.lamp`** (or close). Not `@ldes.ai`. Bio = the `/gloam/` URL.

## How an order actually moves

```
Buyer pays (Gumroad)
    → email with name + address
    → you place the same SKU on the supplier site
      ship-to = the buyer, not you
    → supplier scans tracking
    → you forward tracking to the buyer
```

Until Gumroad is live, the site emails you a **fulfillment ticket**. Do not treat that email as paid. Send a payment link first.

When Gumroad is live, the on-site form is still useful: it copies the ticket so you can paste the address into CJ without retyping. The buyer must enter the **same** address on Gumroad.

## What “making something of it” means

First target is **paid orders that you can fulfill without touching a box**.

- 8 lamps a month × ~€22 left ≈ €176. Small. Real. Proves the pipe.
- Then raise price only if people still buy after seeing the real photos.
- Then — and only then — a second finish (white) as the same SKU family.

If videos get views and zero clicks, the bio link is wrong. If clicks and zero sales, Gumroad is still empty. Fix checkout before you buy ads.

## Do not

- Run Meta/TikTok ads before you have held the sample.
- Promise 48-hour delivery.
- Fake reviews or a countdown stock number.
- Put this in the `@ldes.ai` bio as the main link.
- Sell a no-CE electronics listing into the EU.
