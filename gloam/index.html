    Questions

    Plain answers

    What do I actually get?

    One matte-black sunset lamp and a USB-C cable. It throws a round gold light on a wall. It is not a speaker, a clock, or a smart home hub.

    Is this dropshipping?

    Yes. We don’t keep stock. After you pay, the same lamp is ordered from a supplier and addressed to you. That’s why it takes a week or two, not overnight.

    When will it arrive?

    Plan for 7–14 days inside the EU if the supplier has local stock. If they ship from the factory, it can take longer. Tracking goes out when the carton is scanned.

    Can I return it?

    Yes — 14 days after it arrives, unused, in the same condition, under EU withdrawal rules. Email khourypaul19@gmail.com. Return postage is on you unless the lamp is faulty.

    Why isn’t this on Amazon?

    This shop is one product and one page. If you want a marketplace listing with a thousand twins, that’s not this.
