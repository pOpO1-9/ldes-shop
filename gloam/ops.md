## Before the first sale

1. Pick a supplier with **EU stock** if you can (CJ Dropshipping, Zendrop, Spocket). Search “sunset projection lamp USB-C”. High rating, real order volume.
2. **Buy a sample to yourself.** Site photos are the look, not your carton.
3. Check heat, tilt, USB-C, and the circle on a real wall.
4. **CE / RoHS:** if the sample has no CE mark on the lamp or carton, do not sell it into the EU.
5. Replace `assets/` with photos of *your* sample.
6. Turn on a physical checkout at €39. Paste the URL into `paymentUrl`.

## Order path

```
Buyer pays
  → address in the payment email / dashboard
  → you order the SKU from the supplier, ship-to = the buyer
  → forward tracking
```

If checkout is still email-only, that ticket is **not** paid. Send a payment link first.
