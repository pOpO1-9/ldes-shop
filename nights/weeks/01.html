Week 01 · 18–24 August

Five dinners, one bag.

Feeds 2 · 25–40 min a night · leftovers on Wednesday

Buy the list once. Salt food like an adult. Wednesday is not a new animal — it is Monday’s chicken in a pan with rice and an egg.

The bag

  • Meat / tins Chicken thighs, bone-in if you can — 800 g
    Tuna in olive oil — 1 tin (160 g)
  • Fridge Eggs — 6
    Plain yogurt — 400 g
    Salad greens — 1 bag
    Lemon — 2
    Butter — a small pack if you don’t have it
  • Veg Potatoes — 1 kg
    Carrots — 500 g
    Yellow onions — 3
    Garlic — 1 head
    Frozen peas — 1 small bag
  • Dry Spaghetti or any short pasta — 500 g
    Rice — you need 250 g cooked worth (a mug)
    Red lentils — 250 g
    Canned chopped tomatoes — 1 × 400 g
    Bread — 1 loaf, whatever is decent
  • Assume you have Olive oil, salt, black pepper, paprika.
    If not: buy those once. That is the pantry card later.

The nights

Monday · 40 min · oven 210°C

Tray chicken

Thighs, potatoes in chunks, carrots in batons, two onions in wedges, garlic cloves in their skins. Oil, salt, pepper, paprika. Same tray.

35–40 minutes, until the potatoes give. Eat two thighs tonight with half the veg. Cool the rest of the meat, pick it off the bone, fridge. That is Wednesday.

Tuesday · 20 min

Tuna tomato pasta

Onion and garlic in oil until soft. Tomatoes, salt, a pinch of paprika. Simmer while the pasta cooks. Tuna and a handful of peas in at the end. Lemon.

Pasta water in the pan if it looks tight. Not a cream sauce. Eat.

Wednesday · 20 min

Chicken fried rice

Cold leftover chicken, chopped. Day-old rice if you made extra; if not, cook rice first and spread it on a plate to steam off.

Hot pan, oil, leftover roast veg if any, rice, chicken, peas. Hole in the middle: two eggs scrambled in. Soy if you have it; salt and lemon if you don’t.

Thursday · 30 min

Red lentil soup

Onion, carrot, garlic. Lentils, water to cover by a couple of centimetres, salt. Simmer until the lentils fall apart. Lemon in the bowl, yogurt on top, bread on the side.

If it is too thick, more water. If it is bland, more salt and lemon. That is the whole technique.

Friday · 15 min

Omelette night

Three eggs for two people if you are tired, four if you are not. Remaining salad. Toast from the loaf. Any roast veg still hiding.

Butter in the pan. Don’t fuss the eggs. This is the point of a week card: Friday should be almost nothing.
