# Most Nights — what this shop is

A **new shop**, not a second LDES catalog.

LDES sells things people might want (clips, stills, coins). This shop sells a decision people already make: **what’s for dinner**.

## The product

**Five dinners. One grocery list. Written for two.**

| SKU | Price | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 01 card | Free on the site (proof) | [nights/weeks/01.html](weeks/01.html) — print to PDF |
| This week | €5 | Same format, paid, every Sunday |
| Four weeks | €16 | The real offer. Habit. Repeat revenue |
| The 15 staples | €6 once | Makes every future list shorter |

Not recipes-as-content. Not HelloFresh. A one-page card you put on the fridge.

Week 01 is already written so you are not selling a vibe. You are selling a bag of food and five nights.

## Why this, not lockscreens / clips / soap

- **Everyone eats.** Most people will not mint a coin. Most people will not buy a dream pack. Most people will buy a €5 list that ends the 6pm argument.
- **It repeats.** Income is the Sunday drop, not a one-time pack.
- **You can run it alone.** No warehouse, no EU detergent rules, no Kling budget. Write next week in one evening. Gumroad delivers the PDF.
- **It is not LDES.** Different name, type, audience, TikTok. Do not put this in the @ldes.ai bio as the main link.

Soap, cables, and protein are real needs too — they need suppliers, shipping, and reviews. Skip until this shop has taken a payment.

## TikTok (this is the shop)

You are not posting plating. You are posting **the list**.

Cadence: 5 short videos the first week, then 3 a week. Phone, kitchen table, the printed card.

### Hooks

1. What’s for dinner. Here’s the bag.
2. Five nights. One receipt.
3. Wednesday is leftover chicken. That is the product.
4. I don’t send recipes. I send the list.
5. €5. The week is decided.

### 10 scripts (~12s, last 2s on the card)

1. Dump the grocery bag on the table. Point: chicken, pasta, lentils, eggs. “This is Monday to Friday.”
2. Hold the printed card on the fridge. “This is the shop.”
3. Pan from empty fridge to the list. Cut.
4. “I already know how to cook. I don’t know what to cook.” Card.
5. Receipt vs takeout screenshot. “Five dinners. Less than one delivery.”
6. Wednesday: two thighs into a rice pan. “Paid for on Monday.”
7. Write the grocery list live, 8 items, stop. “That’s the bag.”
8. Someone asks what’s for dinner. Show Friday: omelette. “Most nights.”
9. Four cards in a stack. “This is €16. This is a month of not thinking.”
10. Quiet: salt on chicken, oven door. Text: Week 01, link in bio.

## What “making something of it” means

First target is **repeat Sunday buyers**, not a media brand.

- 10 people × €5/week ≈ €200/month. Boring. Real.
- 20 people on the four-week pack ≈ €320/month without writing a new funnel.
- Then raise price only if people stay.

If the videos get views and zero clicks, the bio link is wrong. If clicks and zero sales, the €5 button is still “coming.” Fix checkout before you write Week 05.
